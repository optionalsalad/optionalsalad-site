DoWeDontWe · by Optional Salad

Making decisions together should feel good.

A calm app for the decisions you make with other people — where to go, what to buy, what to play, what to do. Everyone adds ideas, the group decides in a way that suits the decision, and nobody has to chase a group chat.

Coming soon to Android and iPhone

Who it's for

Friends, families, housemates and small groups who make decisions together: a weekend away, a birthday present, a playlist for the party, a day out, a film for Friday night.

Why we made it

DoWeDontWe was the first app we built. So many choices are now nudged by feeds, rankings and "people also liked", and group decisions get lost in chats where the good ideas scroll away and the loudest voice wins. We wanted one calm place for each decision: your group's own ideas, decided your group's way, with no algorithm telling you what to pick.

What's different

  • Made for deciding, not chatting. Ideas stay in one place instead of scrolling away.
  • Many ways to decide: like, score, rank, swipe, head to head, spin the wheel or keep to a budget.
  • No popularity contests. No trending, no "most liked" lists, nothing nudging you towards what other people chose.
  • Private by default. Rooms are for the people you invite, and nobody else.

How it works

Start a room

One room per decision — a holiday, a birthday, a playlist. Invite people with a code or QR.

Add ideas

Share links from any website or app, or type an idea. Titles, pictures and prices are filled in for you.

Decide your way

Like, score, rank, swipe, go head to head, spin a wheel, or keep a budget — whatever suits the group.

Gifts and plans

Birthday and festive lists keep surprises secret; map rooms plan days out; pocket money rooms keep track of what each child has earned.
