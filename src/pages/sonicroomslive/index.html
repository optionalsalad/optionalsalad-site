Who it's for

Bands and musicians who can't always get everyone into the same room: function bands, covers bands, church and community groups, or a band with a member away for a while. And anyone who just wants to record their own ideas properly.

Why we made it

We play in a band ourselves. Getting everyone together for every rehearsal is hard, and the tools out there let you play together or record properly, but not both in a way a band could actually use afterwards. So we built the rehearsal room we wanted: play live, keep every take, and have every part on everyone's computer when you're done.

What's different

  • A real multitrack recorder you can play live in, not a call with a record button.
  • Everyone gets every part at full quality. Each player records on their own computer, then shares the clean copy with the band.
  • Organised the way bands think: projects, sessions, tracks and takes.
  • Your music never touches our servers. It goes computer to computer.
  • Solo mode too: the same recorder for practising and writing on your own.

How it works

Play live

Up to 16 players in one room, each at home, with a channel each. Your sound goes straight from your computer to theirs — no studio, no server in the middle.

Record every take

Everyone records their own channels at full quality, all starting together. After the take, each part goes to every member and replaces the copy they heard live.

Keep it organised

Projects, sessions, tracks and takes, the way bands already think. Try a new version without losing the one you have.

Fill the gaps

Drummer can't make it? Play last week's drum part from the library. Count-ins, backing tracks and sound effects live in Media.

Practise on your own, too

Solo mode is the same recorder with eight channels, just for you: layer your ideas, play along to a backing track, and copy the best ones to the band when you're ready. Solo needs no internet connection at all.

Everything it does

Playing together

  • Up to 16 players, a channel each
  • Each player's delay and connection quality, shown in words and milliseconds
  • Same house? Play over your home network instead of the internet
  • Players who can't reach each other connect through a bandmate who reaches both
  • Your own mix to hear, or send yours to everyone
  • Hold a key to talk; band chat with quick replies

Recording

  • 16 channels in Band mode, 8 in Solo
  • Audio interfaces with more than two inputs
  • Metronome and count-in, heard but never recorded
  • Every part shared at full quality after each take
  • Missed a rehearsal? Your library catches up next time
  • Projects, sessions, tracks and takes; keep every version

Mixing and playback

  • EQ, compressor, spectrum and master meters
  • Waveform timeline with sections: click to play, drag to loop
  • Backing tracks, count-ins and sound effects in Media
  • Export each part, or bounce a mix
  • Take folders open straight in Audacity, Reaper or GarageBand, lined up
  • Spread across several screens: pop out the channels, timeline, EQ and more

Private and yours

  • Every sound sent between players is encrypted
  • Recordings go computer to computer, never through our servers
  • Bands are invite-only; the leader can hand over, set roles or remove someone
  • Find the lowest delay your computer can manage, in one click
