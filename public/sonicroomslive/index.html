Sonic Rooms Live · by Optional Salad

Rehearse together, wherever you are.

A band rehearsal room and multitrack recorder for your computer. Play live with your bandmates over the internet, record every take, and everyone ends up with every part — in full quality, on their own computer.

Coming soon for Windows · Mac to follow

How it works

Play live

Up to nine players in one room, each at home. Your sound goes straight from your computer to theirs — no studio, no server in the middle.

Record every take

Everyone records their own channels at full quality, all starting together. After the take, each part goes to every member and replaces the copy they heard live.

Keep it organised

Projects, sessions, tracks and takes, the way bands already think. Try a new version without losing the one you have.

Fill the gaps

Drummer can't make it? Play last week's drum part from the library. Count-ins, backing tracks and sound effects live in Media.

A look inside

Screens show an example band and recordings.

Practise on your own, too

Solo mode is the same recorder with eight channels, just for you: layer your ideas, play along to a backing track, and copy the best ones to the band when you're ready.

Built to be calm

  • No ads, and we never sell your data.
  • Your recordings go computer to computer. We never store your music.
  • Bands are invite-only: no public profiles, no discovery, no strangers.
  • Nothing designed to keep you in the app — rehearse, then get on with your day.
