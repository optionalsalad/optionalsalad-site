DoWeDontWe plans

What's free, what Pro adds, and how to change or cancel. Leaving is as easy as joining.

Pro isn't on sale yet. DoWeDontWe is still being tested, and everyone has Pro while it is. Prices will be shown here and in the App Store or Google Play before you buy.

Free

  • 2 active rooms that you created. Archive one to make space. Rooms you join never count, so joining a friend's room always works.
  • The Discovery, Voting, Ranking and When? room types.
  • Joining any room or project, whatever its type.
  • Every safety feature: blocking, reporting, parent controls and deleting your data. These are never paid for.

Pro

  • As many rooms as you like.
  • Every room type: Swipe, Spin the Wheel, Head to Head, Anonymous, Consensus, Budget, Queue, Approval, Game Room, Birthday List, Festive List, Pocket Money and Map.
  • Projects, to keep a group's rooms together as one plan.
  • Exporting results as a message, a PDF, a spreadsheet or an image.

The person who makes a room or project needs Pro. The people they invite join free.

Buying Pro

Pro is bought in the DoWeDontWe app on iPhone, iPad or Android, through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google take the payment, handle renewals and VAT, and send your receipt. You need to be 18 or over to buy.

Pro belongs to your account, not your phone. Sign in with the same Google or Apple account and it works in the web app and the Chrome extension too. You can't buy Pro in the web app or the extension.

Seeing or changing your plan

In the app, tap your avatar (top right of Home) to open Settings. Your plan shows which plan you're on, when it renews and where you bought it. When there's more than one way to pay (monthly or yearly, for example), you can switch there, or in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Apple or Google work out any difference in price.

Cancelling

Cancel in the store you bought Pro from, at least 24 hours before it renews. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the Settings app and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions, then DoWeDontWe.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription.

Or open your Apple subscriptions on the iPhone or iPad. On a Mac or Windows computer, follow Apple's guide to cancelling a subscription.

Android

  1. Open the Google Play app and tap your profile picture.
  2. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap DoWeDontWe, then Cancel subscription.

Or open your Google Play subscriptions on any phone or computer.

In the app, Settings → Your plan → Manage or cancel takes you to the right place.

After you cancel

  • Pro carries on until the end of the time you've paid for, then you're on the free plan.
  • Nothing is deleted. Your rooms, projects and everything in them stay, and the rooms keep working for everyone in them, whatever their type.
  • On the free plan you can't make new Pro rooms or projects or export results. If you have more than 2 active rooms that you created, archive some before making another.

Refunds

Apple and Google handle all refunds for purchases made through them, under their rules. We can't see or refund your payment ourselves. Ask Apple or Google. Your legal rights as a consumer aren't affected.

Questions

Settings → Report a problem in the app, or email support@optionalsalad.com.
