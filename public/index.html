What we believe

Much of today's technology is designed to hold attention. We'd rather make tools that help people get on with their lives — and then get out of the way.

Calm by default

No manufactured urgency, addictive loops or manipulative notifications.

Participation over consumption

Software for making, organising and deciding together — not endless scrolling.

Respect for autonomy

People should think for themselves and decide for themselves, without hidden nudges.

Real life comes first

Our apps should fit into your life, not try to become the centre of it.

Understandable, not opaque

Clear about what our software does and how it handles your data.

Long-term trust

Usefulness and credibility over growth tricks and short-term engagement.
