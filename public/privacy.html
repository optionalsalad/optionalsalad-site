This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, analytics tools or pixels.
  • Everything is served by us. Fonts and styles are hosted on this site, so your browser isn't sent to Google or other third parties when you visit.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver the pages and protect the site from attacks, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. We don't keep our own logs of visitors.
  • Email. If you email us, we receive your email address and message, and use them only to reply and keep a record of the conversation. Our email is forwarded through Cloudflare to Proton Mail. We delete correspondence when it's no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing privacy@optionalsalad.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
